# Antigravity CRM Working Notes

- Intel databank entries must be grounded, concise, and useful. Do not surface raw conversational fragments, profanity-led banter, or bespoke project chatter as standalone intelligence points.
- Every intel point shown in the databank should include meaningful supporting context from the source, with noise and fluff removed rather than repeated.
- Supporting context should preserve the full relevant commercial or personal substance needed for briefing, not a thin generic summary when richer source wording is available.
- When a screenshot clearly represents a WhatsApp chat, email, or other identifiable communication channel, label it by that source channel in the UI instead of the generic label `Image`.
- Do not allow company/project-specific facts from another profile or unrelated source to drift into a person's databank, brief, or relationship trail without direct supporting evidence tied to that profile.
- Do not use speculative relationship filler such as `which could be a rapport-building point`, `may suggest`, or similar soft language in signals or briefs. Keep the wording factual, direct, and evidence-backed.
- Within `Business Focus`, prefer one clear business subtopic per signal when the evidence supports it: `Business Interests`, `Market Pulse`, `Leadership View`, `Commercial Position`, `Strategy & Growth`, or `Operational Pressure`.
